My research combines nonlinear dynamics and control theory, mechanistic modeling, and statistical learning to understand complex biological systems. I study how feedback shapes collective behavior, how neuron–astrocyte interactions support adaptive computation, and how neural population recordings reveal dynamical structure across modes and timescales. Together, these directions connect mathematical analysis with models that explain biological mechanisms and methods that learn interpretable dynamics from data.
